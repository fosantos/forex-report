Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy details how Forex Report manages your information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Forex Report is a static publication site. We do not require you to register, create accounts, or provide personal identity details (such as names or physical addresses) to read our daily market updates. Your access to our content is fully direct and open.

2. Log Files & Analytics

Like most standard websites, we analyze server log information automatically for security and diagnostic purposes. This may include your IP address, browser type, operating system, referencing pages, and visit timestamps. This data is entirely anonymous and used only for traffic statistics.

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

This site displays third-party advertisements served by Google AdSense to fund our research. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites on the Internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to our website.
  • For details on how Google uses information from websites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings page.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Cookie Consent & Your Choices (GDPR / LGPD / California)

On every page that displays ads, we show a Cookie Consent Banner on your first visit. Your choice is stored in your browser (localStorage) and works as follows:

  • Accept All: you allow personalized advertising — Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your interests and prior visits.
  • Decline: you will still see ads (they keep the site free), but they will be non-personalized — we instruct Google not to use cookies for ad personalization on this site.
  • You can review or change your choice at any time via the Cookie Settings link in the footer of any page with ads.

These choices complement your rights under the GDPR (EEA/UK), the LGPD (Brazil) and California law (CCPA/CPRA). The opt-out links in section 3 remain available at all times, regardless of your banner choice.

5. External Resources & Affiliate Links

We may present external links, promotional banners, or affiliate recommendations for trading brokers. Clicking these links redirects you to third-party platforms. We are not responsible for their terms of service, privacy practices, or trading execution. Please perform your own due diligence.

A sua privacidade é extremamente importante para nós. Esta Política de Privacidade detalha como o Forex Report gere as suas informações ao aceder ao nosso site.

O Forex Report é um portal estático e informativo. Não exigimos que os utilizadores façam registos, criem contas ou forneçam quaisquer dados de identidade pessoal (como nomes ou moradas) para aceder às nossas análises diárias. O acesso ao nosso conteúdo é livre e direto.
