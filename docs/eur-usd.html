1. Fundamental & Macro Flow 1. Panorama Fundamentalista & Fluxo Macro

The Euro remains under heavy selling pressure as of June 10, 2026. A blowout US Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP) report on June 5 showing 172,000 jobs created has reignited expectations for a hawkish Fed policy path. Combined with geopolitical uncertainty in the Middle East supporting safe-haven USD flows, the US Dollar Index (DXY) has surged. Meanwhile, growth concerns in the Eurozone continue to leave the fundamental advantage firmly in favor of the greenback, despite expected ECB rate hike of 25 bps to 2.25% on June 11.
O Euro sofre pressão corretiva em 10 de Junho de 2026. Embora o BCE seja amplamente esperado para elevar os juros em 25 pontos-base para 2.25% em 11 de Junho para conter a inflação, a força macroeconômica dos EUA e a proximidade do CPI de hoje mantêm o Dólar dominante.
