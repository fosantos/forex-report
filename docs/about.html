About Us

Welcome to Forex Report, a premium publication offering institution-level analysis and market insights for retail swing and position traders. We believe that professional forex trading should be built on structured, transparent research rather than hype and commercial signals.

Our Methodology

We approach the currency markets by combining three key analytical pillars:

  1. Macroeconomic Context & capital flows: Identifying central bank policies (Hawkish vs. Dovish) to establish long-term directional bias.
  2. Technical Structure: Analyzing clean Price Action (W1 and D1 charts) to find key institutional support/resistance and liquidity pools.
  3. Risk Management: Creating trade configurations with technically protected invalidation levels and an asymmetrical risk-reward ratio of at least 1:2.

Who We Are

Forex Report is an initiative focused on the analytical study of price movements and data in major global currencies. Our goal is to provide structured analysis for free, illustrating in a practical way how technical readings and trading setups are structured day by day.

Nossa Metodologia

Abordamos os mercados de moedas combinando três pilares analíticos cruciais:

  1. Contexto Macro & Fluxo de Capital: Identificamos a postura de Bancos Centrais (Postura Rígida/Hawkish vs Flexível/Dovish) para estabelecer o viés direcional de longo prazo.
  2. Estrutura Técnica: Analisamos o Price Action puro (nos gráficos Diário e Semanal) para mapear zonas críticas de liquidez e suporte/resistência institucional.
  3. Gestão de Risco: Cada operação proposta exige pontos técnicos de invalidação (Stop Loss) claros e uma assimetria matemática de Risco/Retorno mínima de 1:2.
